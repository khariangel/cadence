Cadence 01Khari Angel

Money turns into tokens. Then what?

When I hear "money turns into tokens, tokens turn into finished work," it sounds like "hey presto." Magic. At the same time, it comes with the responsibility of what that actually means. I also find it inspiring and incredibly invigorating, because now I can focus on the things that inspire me: being creative, and ideating what I want to communicate. I think that's the bigger question.

01

What got cheap

I can make a month of emails in an afternoon now, which is kind of hilarious. It changed how I charge. I had to identify what value I'm actually providing, and the value has become strictly what's on the other side: the customer, the audience, the meaning behind the brand that's communicating. That's been the driving force.

02

Messy proof

I think people will crave messy proof of human labor. The messiness, the human mind thinking in real time, will become one of the most appreciated things in the future, because what makes humans so special is our spontaneity.

I think the in-person experience will become more important as we get more used to digital spaces, to the point where we just want to go into the real world. At some point the two will coexist in a way we navigate better. Right now we're still moving through it, on both ends of the spectrum. You're either anti-digital, like a Luddite, or you're pro-digital space.

03

What I tune for

I want to create a space that uses haptic, auditory, and visual mechanisms to create ambient cues with an affective influence on the mind. Not reading too much into it, it's really just creating a vibe. I use different modes of creation, visual and auditory, and my tech stack, what I have, to communicate the vibe I want to communicate.

I've really been into digital solastalgia, and the idea of the digital sanctuary. It's describing and creating an atmosphere, and that's what Sensori Awakened has been for me.

To me, that means creating an atmospheric environment in a digital way. One that provides tranquility, that makes you want to dwell in it, in an ambient way. The closest analogy I have is the video game Life is Strange: an atmospheric, interactive, tranquil environment.

04

When the agent went ahead

The first time I used the Flora MCP, I asked it whether a picture was going to render correctly. Before even acknowledging that it could work, it said it would go ahead and complete the render and show me after. That surprised me. I had never experienced an agent doing that. I was happy with the results in the end, and it was a cheap render too.

That's another thing about this agentic vanguard. Because the agents are getting so knowledgeable, you can layer them. You can add a QC agent to look over your copy or creative. It's becoming an even bigger closed loop, and I think it will keep expanding and get richer in context, in understanding, in direction.

05

What could be wrong

Tokens may not be the new form of currency in the future. I don't know what will be. Money turns into tokens, tokens are spent on autonomous creation, and the new frontier of agentic tools complicates it further. It's a kind of liminal space I wouldn't even know how to describe. But I'm fascinated by how meta it is next to how we describe value, and what we ascribe to work in general.
